Blog posts go here as .md files, one per post. There are none yet, on purpose: until the first real post is added,
the blog index is hidden from search engines, left out of the sitemap, and not linked from anywhere.
Write posts by hand from real experience (see BLOG-OUTLINES.md for what each one should cover).

Frontmatter every post needs (the checks in src/content.config.ts reject a post without it):

  ---
  title: "What I Charge for Mobile Detailing in DFW"
  description: "One or two sentences for search results (under 160 characters)."
  date: 2026-11-01
  updated: 2026-11-01        # optional, change it when you update the post
  author: "Brock Livermore"  # optional, defaults to Brock Livermore
  ---
